# Primary UI/UX Design System: Taste-Skill & Impeccable Craft Protocol

This rule governs **ALL frontend, UI, styling, and design work** across the entire repository.
Every visual component, page layout, interaction, and styling choice must strictly follow the combined philosophy and execution standards of **Taste-Skill** (`@taste-skill`) and **Impeccable** (`@impeccable`).

---

## 💎 Core Directives: The Anti-Slop & Impeccable Craft Floor

1. **Zero Generic AI Slop (Anti-Default Discipline)**:
   - **Banned**: Lazy generic templates, cliché purple/pink mesh gradients, three identical feature cards with generic icons, raw Inter on dull slate with no typographic character.
   - **Required**: Distinct, deliberate art direction tailored to AuraBook (Luxury tech publishing: Pure crisp whites, deep ocean blues, warm gilded amber accents, tactile depth, subtle glassmorphism, and editorial typography).

2. **The 4 Operating Modes (Impeccable Framework)**:
   - **Persuade (Storefront, Landing, Detail Pages)**: Earn visitor attention and action with striking visual hierarchy, compelling micro-interactions, and premium typography.
   - **Operate (Admin Portal, CRM, Order Management, Vouchers, DRM)**: Extreme scanability, data density, rapid action triggers, zero visual noise, crystal-clear state badges, and resilient tables.
   - **Read (DRM E-Book Reader, Documentation)**: Optimal legibility, calm canvas framing, zero distractions, reader comfort.
   - **Experience (3D Audio Teaser, Voice AI, Interactive Cards)**: Delightful physics, fluid gestures, and sensory feedback.

3. **The 3 Dials Configuration (Taste-Skill)**:
   - `DESIGN_VARIANCE: 7` (Asymmetric visual rhythm, unexpected grid breaks, distinct focal points)
   - `MOTION_INTENSITY: 6` (Smooth spring transitions, tactile button depressions, fluid drawer slides, no gratuitous infinite spins)
   - `VISUAL_DENSITY: 4` for Storefront / `7` for Admin Dashboard (Clear information hierarchy with room to breathe vs. compact power-user efficiency)

4. **Brand Consistency & Official Assets**:
   - The official AuraBook brand emblem (`/logo.png`, based on the geometric cinematic aerial motif) must be utilized consistently in all brand touchpoints (Navbar, Footer, Auth pages, Admin header).
   - Maintain the refined brand palette:
     - Primary: Deep Ocean Blue (`#0284c7`, `#0369a1`, `#0c4a6e`)
     - Accent: Warm Gold / Amber (`#d97706`, `#f59e0b`, `#fbbf24`)
     - Neutral: Clean crisp slate & pure white background
     - Semantic: Emerald (Success/Paid/Active), Rose (Revoked/Failed), Amber (Pending/Warning)

5. **Completeness & Bounded Polish**:
   - Never leave placeholder boxes, missing states, unhandled loading, or broken error boundaries.
   - Both Mobile & Desktop viewports must be responsive, touch-friendly, and visually harmonious.
